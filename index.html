What this is

A reference book on the claims most often made against or about Islam — from "spread by the sword" and "jihad means holy war" to Qur'an 4:34, Aisha's age, sharia, and the "moon god" theory. Each claim is answered from the sources, then footnoted to a numbered entry in the bibliography.

The label "misconception" is itself contested. Where there is real scholarly dispute — the military dimension of jihad, apostasy, Qur'an 4:34, Aisha's age, and slavery — the book says so plainly and gives both sides, rather than presenting one view as settled.
